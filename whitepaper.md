# Q ROADMAP

## What is Q?

Q is a geo-fenced fast admission and queueing system for the modern day nightlife goer.
Q utilizes geo-location and geo-fencing technology as well as Q-user databases to create real life, real-time Queues for bars and pubs, or other businesses that require time sensitive visits, avoiding the congestion and sometimes uncomfortable experience of standing in lines and bringing the convenience of a restaurant buzzer to your smartphone. This is to emphasize not only on leisure and to accentuate the positivity of an evening by being able to have all events flow seamlessly into one another, but also to create an ecosystem in which businesses can harvest their own real-time data about user behaviour and preferences through a variety of corporate landscapes.

This presentation will focus solely on the application of the service to the Nightlife industry, as it was the first theorized industry when Q was in its blueprint stages.
The service however can be applied to a multitude of corporate environments including but not limited to: Barbershops, Mechanic appointments, pharmacy visits and doctors appointments.
It would allow for users to have a DIGITAL reminder system for their professional obligations while also monitoring the likelihood for said user to commit infractions such as No-shows or indecent behavior.

By using already existing infrastructure like Apple Maps and Google Maps, we will draw the boundaries of businesses on top of the satellite imagery provided to us. A second option to create a boundary would be a NEAR FIELD COMMUNICATION or NFC type system, integrated into the smartphone or device of an employee within the confines of said business. The device would project a Bluetooth near-field radius that would create a confining digital boundary that represents the business itself.

This boundary creates a GEO FENCED area upon which we are aware of how many occupants are inside of the building.
This is great user data for the bars, pubs and other businesses, constantly having a REAL TIME representation of how many people are inside the bar/club/pub and/or other business, it makes it easy to stay within the constraints of fire capacity and/or COVID capacities, as well as time constraints for places where time is a sensitive commodity.

The applications however move much further for the end user than just a real-time occupant counter.
In order to use Q, businesses would have to set aside anywhere from 10-50 places in their capacity ordnance specifically for the Q-users, depending on the size of capacity.

Users will be verified using a Selfie + ID upload to confirm that they are who they say they are and will be listed as Q-USERS on our service. Using a QR code or a click link via the provided map, Q-users can enter the "Q", meaning they are next in line to enter the building.

Q-users can also use this feature much like UBER to RESERVE seating or space at said venue.
Q-Users will link banking information to Q to use to pay cover or entrance fees and Q will pay out to the venue bi-weekly after issuing a lowly 1% service fee on total dollars collected.
For every $5 collected, Q takes 5 cents. This money is to help maintain our infrastructure, pay wages and to promote the application. We view this as a fair exchange to use our cutting edge services. This payment model could be applied to just about any business that uses our service to schedule appointments, visits, package reception, or table reservation.

The possibilities for Q's application are literally endless and could revolutionize the way businesses and consumers or patrons interact with one another, giving both sides the ability to score their experience with the other.

## WHAT THE SERVICE INCLUDES:

"Q" creates not only a digital map for businesses to advertise themselves on (for free), a queueing program to allow users and businesses to interact and schedule with one another digitally and universally, but also, a secure digital database where businesses can score their users based on merit and conduct, creating another web of safety not only for venue staff but also patrons. Users will have a default score of 100 and for every infraction they will be docked score depending on the seriousness of said infraction.

Used in an example: If a user has a good score and one night has a little too much to drink and has to be asked to leave, their profile is given a mark based off of that behaviour and they are docked score. If the next time the user shows up to the bar and no mark is given to their profile they will automatically return to the score they originally had, which is 100, or good standing everywhere.
The lower your score goes however, the longer it takes for it to climb back up to its original standing.

To extend upon this feature, venue staff could issue bans that would alert them when a restricted user has crossed the vicinity of the geo-fence. The service would also alert the user they are not welcome in the venue and to contact the venue staff to clear the ban.
If a negligent user is banned several times from several places they will be issued a red flag by the service.
This will alert staff to a potential misdemeanor or incident with said flagged user.
Good behavior and manual clearing from venue staff will allow users to drop their flagged status.

## HOW IT WORKS:

Users sign up to enter a venue Q and then go about their evening. Say a club is at capacity but there is a local DJ you want to see.
You and your friends enter the Q and then go to another bar, or get food, or really do anything other than stand in a line. When the capacity has shifted and users or patrons exit the bar, your spot in the line will be moved further up the Q. Once you hit the top of the line you will be given a notification with a 1 Minute, 30 second timer (could vary depending on distance to venue).

If the Q-User does not meet the time limit they will be added to the back of the "Q".

Extending upon this feature could be done in several ways, one of which would be pre-booking arrival times within grace periods.
To be used in an example; A user would book arrival at 10:45, pre-paying party of 5. Party of 5 verified Q-USERS with all included picture IDs would arrive on the end-user software available to venue staff and security or doorstaff.
This would allow seamless transition into the venue to a pre-booked space that has already been paid for, completely avoiding the experience of standing in line. The service would give the Q-USER a 15-30 minute grace period, agreed upon in advance by the venue as part of their venue parameters. If the Q-USER does not arrive within that time period they are penalized with a move to the back of the line.

This process can be applied to many other businesses as well. A doctor's appointment would be easily booked and if the office is in a shared building or shopping center like many of the offices in the city of Q's origins, it would allow patients to go about their day and continue shopping while they wait for their doctor to be ready. The patient would then have X amount of time to enter the waiting room or else be docked points on their timeliness in this case. In other applications the score may be given to other traits such as behavior or even likeliness to spend money in the sense of use in a casino. As earlier stated, the applications of Q are quite literally endless.

The PayPal method. 1% of total revenue that passes through our system we feel is a fair percent to use our cutting edge software and services. On top of that, Q will offer business RELEVANT advertising space to their business and user interest as well as frequency of proximity to said business. Q expects 5 million users in its first year of launch, the ad revenue from 5 million users will at first be small but it will be funnelled back into the company to be used for promotion of the service and system.

Q as an organization has an aggressive marketing plan and business acumen, poised to corner an extremely large share of the market post Nightlife industry roll out. We plan to integrate ourselves into every business possible and make our name not only a noun, but a verb as well. Much like tweeting has become the verb of Twitter, Q'ing aims to be the verb of Q. Corporate ecosystems leave potential for a much larger influx of revenue as well as benefit to both the user and the business using our services. Q^b (to the power of business) would be the second phase of the Q system rollout and perhaps the most successful in generating revenue for shareholders and the company alike.

Services like Q and Q^b exist in China/Japan/Korea solely where technology rules the modern day business transaction.
These services have not caught on in North America because they are not user friendly or easy to use without comprehension of the Chinese language. Security and privacy concerns are also an issue when dealing with companies of foreign origin as data is a very sensitive commodity.
Q aims to be at the pinnacle of security, transparency and privacy.
One of the goals of Q is to reward users for allowing us to give businesses a place to advertise to them digitally. An unspecified % of user specific ad revenue will be funnelled back into the account of Q-Users to act as a reward for using the application as well as to be used to pay for whatever service or business their account may be linked to.

An extra model of revenue that Q may pull from is the premium features of the services available.
Q-Users may pay for extra time to add to their "GRACE PERIOD" or "ARRIVAL PERIOD", this revenue may be divided amongst the venues and businesses for which the "EXTRA TIME" is added. Q may offer anywhere from 10-50% of revenue from PREMIUM FEATURES to said business, depending on volume of traffic and business.
